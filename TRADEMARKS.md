## What you can do without asking

- Say that your application or service uses MentorAgent, or is built with MentorAgent.
- Write articles, tutorials, books, talks and videos about MentorAgent, even with its name in the title.
- Redistribute the unmodified packages and source code under their own names, with the license and NOTICE files.
- Describe an extension as working with MentorAgent ("for MentorAgent", "compatible with MentorAgent"), as long as
  the name of your product is your own.
- Use the logo, unmodified, next to text that refers to the MentorAgent project, for example in a list of the
  technologies you use.

## What needs our permission

- Using "MentorAgent" or the logo in the name of a product, a service, a company, a domain or a social media
  account.
- Publishing a package whose ID or name contains "MentorAgent", for example on NuGet or npm.
- Using the logo on merchandise, or in a modified form.
- Offering MentorAgent as a hosted service under the MentorAgent name.

## What you cannot do

- Call your product "MentorAgent", "MentorAgent Console", or a name that can be confused with them.
- Distribute a modified version of MentorAgent under the MentorAgent name. If you change the code, give your
  version another name; you can still say it is "based on MentorAgent".
- Suggest that we make, endorse, certify, sponsor or support your product or service when we do not.
- Use the marks in a way that is misleading, or that damages the reputation of the project.

## Asking for permission, reporting misuse

Write to info@mentoragent.net: say who you are, which name or logo you want to use, and how. Write to the same
address if you see the marks used in a way this policy does not allow. Security vulnerabilities go to
security@mentoragent.net instead (see SECURITY.md).
